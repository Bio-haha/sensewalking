Projects

Our art projects emerge from ongoing dialogues between biology, technology, and everyday life. Each work functions as an experiment in perception, blending installation, sound, and participatory process to explore how human and non-human systems co-resonate. Rather than fixed outcomes, these projects are open frameworks for interaction, reflection, and play.
