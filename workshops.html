Workshops

Workshops and collaborations form the connective tissue of Biohaha’s practice. They open spaces for collective experimentation, where knowledge is built through shared action, discussion, and improvisation. Whether through sensewalking, DIY lab-building, or sound-making, these encounters translate research into accessible, participatory formats.

Dudul Muni

Dudul Muni (touch and sound) is a workshop program initiated by Biohaha. Here, the participants will learn how capacitive sensor works using microcontrollers and other electronic components. Capacitive touch sensing is a way of human touch sensing, that requires no force to activate. It is used to experience human touch through almost any kind of material. In this workshop, we use biotic materials where the participants assemble a simple circuit to call voice data using plant media. This circuit can also act as a controller for other media such as video, lights, and other mechanical equipment.
